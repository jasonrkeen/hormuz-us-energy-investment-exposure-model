# Cycle Normalization Methodology

## Purpose
Test whether the apparent 2026 Hormuz beneficiaries remain compelling if refining margins revert toward pre-shock / mid-cycle company economics.

## VLO
Q2 2026 total refining margin: $23.62/bbl.
Normalized anchor: approximately $11.47/bbl, based on 2024–2025 full-year refining margin dollars divided by throughput volumes.

## MPC
Q2 2026 R&M margin: $36.33/bbl.
Historical margins:
- 2023: $23.00
- 2024: $16.01
- 2025: $16.87
Average: $18.63/bbl.

## PSX
Q2 2026 worldwide realized refining margin: $24.08/bbl.
Historical:
- 2024: $8.84
- 2025: $10.88
Average: $9.86/bbl.

## Translation
For each scenario:

`change in segment earnings proxy = (scenario margin - Q2 margin) × Q2 throughput × 91 days`

This simple bridge intentionally keeps the sensitivity transparent.

## Major caveat
The three companies report different segment metrics. The outputs measure *earnings retention within each issuer*, not absolute cross-company earnings comparability.
