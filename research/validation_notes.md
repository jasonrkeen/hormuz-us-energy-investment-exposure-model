# Validation Notes — v0.4

- Valuation snapshot dates range from August 26-28, 2026.
- Forward P/E depends on consensus analyst earnings estimates.
- FCF yield is trailing/current rather than a forecast.
- EV/EBITDA is sensitive to unusually high or low refining margins.
- Refining is cyclical; today's low forward P/E can reflect expectations for elevated near-term earnings rather than permanent undervaluation.
- Net debt/EBITDA is included to distinguish financial resilience from simple cheapness.
- A prospective score is provided because VLO, MPC, and PSX have already experienced very large 2026 equity gains.
- Before publication, refresh prices/valuation and test normalized mid-cycle EBITDA assumptions.
