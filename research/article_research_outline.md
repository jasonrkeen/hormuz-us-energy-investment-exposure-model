# Article Research Outline

## Working title
**The Hormuz Trade Has Already Paid Off: Why Valero May Still Have the Edge**

## Central research question
After large 2026 gains in U.S. refining equities, which company still offers the strongest combination of Hormuz-disruption exposure, current valuation and resilience to refining-margin normalization?

## Suggested structure

### 1. Investment thesis
- The thesis is not simply “oil prices rise during conflict.”
- The key mechanism is the relative scarcity of refined products and alternative refining capacity.
- VLO currently ranks first in the model; MPC is the strongest competing thesis.

### 2. What changed at Hormuz
- Establish the decline in Hormuz flows using EIA data.
- Explain why crude, refined products, freight, logistics and regional refinery availability matter.

### 3. Crack spreads show a regime change
Use `outputs/crack_regime_summary.csv`.

Key averages:
- Jan–Feb: $24.15/bbl
- Mar–Apr: $47.24/bbl
- May–Jun: $52.25/bbl
- Jul–Aug: $65.55/bbl

### 4. Equity-market confirmation
Use `outputs/market_performance.csv`.

2026 sample returns:
- MPC: 125.94%
- VLO: 116.31%
- PSX: 90.79%
- XLE: 39.20%
- CVX: 33.10%
- XOM: 30.33%

### 5. Compare VLO, MPC and PSX
Discuss:
- operating exposure
- current valuation
- FCF yield
- leverage
- market confirmation
- sensitivity to normalized refining margins

### 6. Why VLO ranks first
Live prospective score:
- VLO: 76.06
- MPC: 64.12
- PSX: 43.52

Avoid presenting the score as a price target. Explain the components and relative interpretation.

### 7. The Marathon counterargument
This section is essential.
- MPC produced the highest equity return in the sample.
- MPC has the highest modeled cycle-resilience score.
- MPC retains slightly more segment earnings under mid-cycle and soft-cycle scenarios.
- Higher leverage is an important offset.

### 8. Normalization risk
Use `outputs/cycle_normalization_scenarios.csv` and `cycle_adjusted_refiner_ranking.csv`.

Final cycle-adjusted scores:
- VLO: 77.66
- MPC: 74.88
- PSX: 30.46

### 9. What could invalidate the thesis?
- rapid Hormuz normalization
- sharp crack-spread compression
- recession / product-demand destruction
- refinery outages or utilization problems
- changes in crude differentials
- operating-cost inflation
- valuation rerating beyond fundamentals
- analyst earnings estimates falling faster than price

### 10. Conclusion
The research supports VLO as the strongest overall relative exposure in the tested universe, with MPC close behind once cycle normalization is emphasized.
